Mission and Architecture

Engineering safer skies for autonomous aviation

SkyClear was created to solve pre-flight airspace authorization, live fleet monitoring, and statutory compliance for commercial and recreational drone operations in India.

The Challenge

Bridging pilots and aviation authorities

As unmanned aviation grows across delivery, survey, agriculture, and infrastructure, airspace safety requires transparent rules, instant clearance decisions, and proactive geofence monitoring.

01

Deterministic Safety

In safety-critical aviation, decisions cannot rely on unpredictable models. SkyClear runs strict mathematical polygon intersections against official boundaries before any rule explanation is provided.

02

Real-time Deconfliction

Airspace restrictions change dynamically with VIP movements and emergency operations. SkyClear links active flight permits with live NOTAM publishing, alerting pilots in the air within seconds.

03

Statutory Compliance

Every decision links directly to the DGCA Drone Rules 2021, Ministry of Civil Aviation circulars, and local airfield advisories, ensuring full regulatory clarity for pilots and authorities alike.

04

Open Architecture

Built with modular REST APIs, standard Leaflet mapping, ArduPilot and MAVLink telemetry adapters, and local LLM acceleration, enabling offline operation and easy integration.

Engineering Stack

System architecture and performance specifications

Subsystem Technology Role and Guarantee
Geospatial Engine Shapely, GeoJSON, Leaflet Sub-millisecond point-in-polygon checks and 100 m corridor route sampling.
Fleet Simulation Python asyncio, Kinematics 100+ concurrent drone simulation with forward 30-second breach projection.
Hybrid Storage SQLite, MongoDB Time-series Relational schemas for permits paired with 24-hour auto-expiring telemetry TTL.
Regulatory RAG Ollama, Local Embeddings Deterministic rule citations from statutory civil aviation documents without external leakage.
Authentication JWT, Google GIS, TOTP Multi-tier role access: self-registered pilots and invite-only officials with MFA.
