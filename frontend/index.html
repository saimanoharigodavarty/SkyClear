Can I fly my drone here?

    SkyClear checks a spot against airport, defence and other restricted areas in your area and answers in plain words, with the rule behind it. If you need permission, it goes to the right officer.

    1. Pick a spot on the map, or search for a place.
    2. Choose your drone and time.
    3. Press Check and read the answer.

    Plan a route

    Click the map to add waypoints. SkyClear checks every 100 m of the route, then flies a simulated drone along it so you can see where the trouble is.

    Click the map to add waypoints, at least 2.

    Ask about the rules

    Ask in plain words. The answer comes only from the rule passages we hold, and the rule is shown so you can check it. If the rules do not cover your question, it says so.
