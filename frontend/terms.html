What SkyClear is

SkyClear is a pre-flight planning aid. It shows zones, notices and rule passages near a point you choose and gives a verdict based on the data it holds.

SkyClear is decision support only. It does not grant, replace or prove permission to fly. "No restriction found in our data" does not mean a flight is allowed or safe. Always confirm with the official portal and local authorities before flying.

Data

Rules, zones and notices in this version are sample or derived data and may be incomplete, outdated or wrong. Each answer shows its sources and the date the data was last updated.
